FAQ

Is this the same as the R$ 799 fast website?

No. Fast websites are templates for presence. E-commerce is the operation: brand, domain, checkout, payments, shipping, labels, languages and admin. See fast websites.

Which payments do you connect?

In Brazil: PIX, card and Mercado Pago. Abroad: PayPal and Stripe. Checkout uses the right rail per market.

Do shipping and labels come included?

Yes — Correios, Jadlog, Super Frete and, when it fits, Uber Direct, with label generation/print in admin.

Can the site be in multiple languages?

Yes — PT, EN, IT or any project language, with mirrored structure and SEO per language.

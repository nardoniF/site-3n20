FAQ

What does 3n20 sell, in one sentence?

We build product, brand and e-commerce: name, slogan, logo, domain, patent, website, own store, marketplaces, Ads and AI.

Do you create slogan, logo and domain?

Yes. We find the name, buy the domain, create logo and slogan, and register the brand when it makes sense.

National and international patents?

Yes — INPI in Brazil and strategy/filing abroad. Scope goes in the quote.

Do you set up Mercado Livre, Shopee, Amazon…?

Yes — Mercado Livre, Shopee, Amazon, Magazine Luiza and TikTok Shop, plus your own store on the site.

What is SEO / AI analysis?

A site and content audit: what ranks better, what engages, what’s missing on the pages. We also deliver chatbots and AI interactions on the site.

Do you configure Ads?

Yes — campaign structure, pixel/conversions and alignment with store and funnel.
